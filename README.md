# GoWild Tracker

Static prototype for a Frontier GoWild trip planner, with an added DC-area view.

The default DC-area view lists dated one-way fare cards captured from Frontier's public city pages for DCA, IAD, and BWI on Nov 19, 2026. Frontier describes these as Discount Den Basic fares found by other travelers in the prior 48 hours. These are snapshots, not live prices, GoWild fares, or confirmed itineraries; click through to Frontier to check current offers and routing.

The separate demo search uses a fabricated timetable: origin, destination or anywhere, today or tomorrow, nonstop or one connection (75 minute minimum). Flight numbers, schedules, and seat counts are illustrative only. It does not call Frontier and must not be used to make travel decisions.

Frontier's published GoWild terms describe a domestic booking window starting the day before departure and an international window starting 10 days before departure (Puerto Rico is treated as domestic). Pass terms, fees, and availability can change; check Frontier's current terms and booking flow.

As checked Nov 19, 2026, Frontier is advertising its 2027 Annual Pass as having no blackout dates; limited GoWild availability and separate peak-day charges still apply.

Sources checked Nov 19, 2026:

- [Frontier flights from Washington, D.C. (DCA/IAD)](https://flights.flyfrontier.com/en/flights-from-washington)
- [Frontier flights from Baltimore (BWI)](https://flights.flyfrontier.com/en/flights-from-baltimore)
- [GoWild Pass](https://www.flyfrontier.com/deals/gowild-pass/)
- [Frontier route map](https://www.flyfrontier.com/travel/my-trips/route-map/?mobile=true)
- [Frontier destinations FAQ](https://faq.flyfrontier.com/help/what-cities-does-frontier-fly-to)

The route map shows where Frontier serves over its network, not which routes operate on a particular date or where GoWild seats are available. Connections in this prototype are separate illustrative legs, not a guaranteed through-itinerary. Live seat search would need a separate data service; booking stays on Frontier.
