GoWild Tracker

Search sample Frontier-style GoWild seats, including one-stop connections. This page does not talk to Frontier. Live inventory needs a separate worker; GitHub Pages can only host the board.

What this starter is

  • A route search over a small bundled timetable: origin, destination or anywhere, today or tomorrow, nonstop or one connection.
  • Connections need the same airport and at least 75 minutes between arrival and the next departure. Seats shown are the tighter leg.
  • A later worker would refresh real availability inside the GoWild booking window and replace this sample file. Booking stays on Frontier.
