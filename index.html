Current GoWild notes

  • GoWild is capacity-controlled: a Frontier route or operating flight does not guarantee a GoWild seat. Confirm schedules and book directly with Frontier.
  • Frontier's published pass terms describe domestic booking beginning the day before departure and international booking beginning 10 days before departure. Puerto Rico is treated as domestic. Availability, fees, and restrictions can change; check the terms for your pass and trip.
  • Frontier currently advertises a 2027 Annual Pass with no blackout dates, but GoWild seats remain limited and peak-day charges may apply. No blackout dates does not mean a seat is guaranteed.
  • This prototype still searches a bundled demo timetable only. It does not query Frontier, and none of the displayed seat counts or flight numbers are real.
  • Connections are a planning aid, not a through-ticket: allow time for separate segments, and verify whether you must collect/recheck bags or clear customs.

Information checked Nov 19, 2026. Sources: Frontier GoWild Pass terms · Frontier route map · Frontier destinations FAQ.
